# Badge Dot

Indicator with no number: new or unread.

Size: Small 6, Medium 8 (`rounded-full`). Tone: Neutral, Strong (`bg/inverse`), Info, Success, Warning, Danger (`feedback/<tone>/dot`). Used in Status (the tone dot) and Card Header.
